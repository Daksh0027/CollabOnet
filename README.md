# CollabOnet

> **Connecting & Management Platform for Social Media Agencies, Creators, and Brand Sponsors**

CollabOnet is a zero-trust, human-in-the-loop collaboration platform designed to eliminate scams and corruption caused by unauthorized middlemen and mediators in influencer marketing.

---

## 🚀 Key Features

- **2-Phase Verification Pipeline**:
  - **Phase 1 (Automated)**: Platform handle proof, corporate domain/phone OTP, and in-browser webcam liveness video recording.
  - **Phase 2 (Human-in-the-Loop)**: Admin review queue & 1-on-1 WebRTC video verification call before marketplace access is granted.
- **Dual-Access Privacy & Messaging**:
  - **Creator View**: Direct communication with verified brand points of contact.
  - **Manager View**: Company-level view for budget negotiation, protecting creators while preserving operational boundaries.
- **Digital Deal Signing & Cryptographic Escrow**:
  - Automated PDF agreement generation with SHA-256 document hashing.
  - Milestone unlock system (Script $\to$ Rough Cut $\to$ Published Post verification).
- **Calendar & Deliverables Tracker**:
  - Timeline view for upcoming post deliverables and walk-in event RSVPs.
- **Creator-to-Creator "Collab Tab"**:
  - Filter and match with fellow creators by niche and reach for peer collaborations.
- **"Hall of Shame" Public Fraud Registry**:
  - Evidentiary dispute resolution and public accountability registry for contract defaulters and fraudulent mediators.
